Install StarterApp

Install StarterApp on your iPhone for free. No App Store listing or paid Apple Developer account is required.

Open StarterApp

Finish installation on iPhone

  1. Open this page in Safari on your iPhone.
  2. Tap Open StarterApp, then tap Safari's Share button.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen.
  4. Enable Open as Web App if iOS shows that option, then tap Add.

What this installs: a removable Home Screen web app that can work offline after its first load. It is not a native iOS app and does not require device management, a certificate, or access to personal data.

This works from the published HTTPS site. A local localhost preview will not be reachable from your phone.
